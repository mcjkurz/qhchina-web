Part of **Helper Utilities** (`qhchina.helpers.fonts.list_remote_fonts`).

**Returns:**
List of dicts with font information:
[{'file': 'NotoSansTCSC-Regular.otf', 'size': 17279824, 'size_mb': 16.5}, ...]
