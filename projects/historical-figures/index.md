# Historical Figures (HistFig)

<img src="main.webp" alt="Historical Figures" style="max-width: 80%; height: auto; margin: 2rem auto; display: block;">

HistFig is an AI-enhanced chatbot system for simulating conversations with key figures from Chinese history. Built with FastAPI and leveraging Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), the system combines vector search using ChromaDB for semantic similarity, BM25 lexical search for keyword matching, and Reciprocal Rank Fusion to ground AI responses in historical documents—speeches, essays, and letters. Implemented in both undergraduate and graduate courses at Lingnan University, HistFig enabled students to converse with AI-simulated personas of Li Hongzhang, Lu Xun, Sun Yat-sen, Qiu Jin, Gu Hongming, Mao Zedong, Deng Xiaoping, and others.
