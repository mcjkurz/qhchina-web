{%- comment -%} Finds the counterpart of the current page in the other language (same `ref`). Sets `lang_other` and `lang_alt` (nil for pages without a translation, e.g. docs). {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign lang_alt = nil -%} {%- if page.lang == 'zh' -%}{%- assign lang_other = 'en' -%}{%- else -%}{%- assign lang_other = 'zh' -%}{%- endif -%} {%- if page.ref -%} {%- assign lang_candidates = site.pages | concat: site.projects | where: "ref", page.ref -%} {%- assign lang_alt = lang_candidates | where: "lang", lang_other | first -%} {%- endif -%}
